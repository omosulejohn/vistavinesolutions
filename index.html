The Offer

The Security & Compliance Gap Assessment

One senior-led assessment, mapped to both SOC 2 and ISO 27001. The two frameworks share many controls, so you pay once and see where you stand against each.

What you get

  • A control-by-control gap matrix against both frameworks
  • A findings report with severity ratings
  • A prioritized 90-day roadmap with effort estimates
  • A one-page executive summary you can share with leadership and customers
  • A 60-minute readout with your team

How it works

  1. Week 1Kickoff, document review, and access set-up.
  2. Week 2Stakeholder interviews and a read-only review of your cloud configuration.
  3. Week 3Report delivery and readout.

What this isn't

This is a readiness assessment, not an audit or attestation. SOC 2 reports come from an independent CPA firm, and ISO 27001 certificates from an accredited certification body. We tell you when you're ready and introduce audit partners. Penetration testing and remediation are scoped separately.

Fixed fee, 2 to 3 weeks. The fee is credited toward a readiness program or advisory retainer if you continue within 30 days.

FAQ

Questions we hear often

Do we need SOC 2 or ISO 27001?

It depends on who is asking. US customers most often ask for SOC 2, while international and enterprise buyers often ask for ISO 27001. The assessment maps your gaps to both, so you can choose with evidence.

Is the assessment an audit?

No. It shows where you stand and what to fix. SOC 2 reports must come from an independent CPA firm, and ISO 27001 certificates from an accredited certification body. We introduce audit partners when you're ready.

What access do you need to our systems?

Read-only access to cloud configuration and documentation, agreed in writing before we start. We do not change your systems during an assessment.

How long does it take?

Two to three weeks from kickoff to readout, depending on the size of your environment and how quickly we get access and interviews scheduled.

How much does it cost?

A fixed fee, quoted after a short call once we know your size and scope. The fee is credited toward follow-on work if you continue within 30 days.

Do you do penetration testing?

Not as part of the assessment. When you need it, we help scope it and coordinate an independent testing firm, so the results feed straight into your roadmap.
