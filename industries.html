Where We Focus

Five industries, one recurring problem

B2B SaaS & Technology

Enterprise customers increasingly require a SOC 2 report before they'll sign. It's often the single biggest blocker on a late-stage deal, and the one most founders underestimate.

Fintech & Payments

Banking partners, card networks, and regulators all expect a documented security and compliance posture — not optional for companies moving money.

Healthcare & Healthtech

Patient data handling draws HIPAA-adjacent scrutiny the moment you scale, especially when expanding into the US or working with larger health systems.

Logistics & Supply Chain

Enterprise retail and shipping partners increasingly run vendor security reviews before onboarding a new logistics provider into their systems.

Insurance & Professional Services

Client trust and regulatory obligations both demand a documented, defensible security program — and clients are increasingly willing to ask for proof.
