Services

A full security and compliance bench, built one engagement at a time

Most clients start with the Gap Assessment. These are the services it can lead into, brought in only as you need them — never sold as an all-or-nothing package.

Start here

Security & Compliance Gap Assessment

A control-by-control view of where you stand against SOC 2 and ISO 27001, with a prioritized roadmap to close the gaps. Fixed fee, 2 to 3 weeks.

  • Control-by-control gap matrix against both frameworks
  • Findings report with severity ratings
  • Prioritized 90-day roadmap with effort estimates
  • 60-minute readout with your team

Cloud Security Architecture Review

A hands-on review of your AWS, Azure, or GCP environment — identity and access, network boundaries, storage, and logging — against real-world misconfigurations, not just a checklist.

  • Architecture review across IAM, network, storage, and logging
  • Concrete hardening recommendations, prioritized by risk
  • Optional hands-on implementation support

SOC 2 & ISO 27001 Readiness Program

For teams that are further along: policy development, control implementation, and evidence collection to get genuinely audit-ready, with a trusted auditor introduced when you are.

  • Policy and procedure development
  • Control implementation support
  • Evidence collection and audit-trail setup
  • Introduction to an independent audit partner

Third-Party & Vendor Risk Management

A vendor inventory, risk tiering, and a repeatable process — so the next time a customer asks about your subprocessors, you have a real answer ready.

  • Full vendor and subprocessor inventory
  • Risk tiering and review cadence
  • A repeatable process for answering customer vendor questionnaires

Fractional CISO Retainer

Ongoing security leadership on a capped monthly retainer — for companies that need senior judgment regularly, but not a full-time hire.

  • Monthly security and risk reviews
  • Direct support on customer security questionnaires
  • Ongoing guidance for your internal team
  • Capped hours, so cost stays predictable

Incident Response Planning

A documented response plan and a tabletop exercise, so if a real incident happens, your team isn't improvising for the first time under pressure.

  • Documented incident response plan with defined roles
  • Tabletop exercise with your team
  • Post-exercise gap review
